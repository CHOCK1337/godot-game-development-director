# Content Team Orchestration Example

Wave 1: Content Production establishes the slice and cut boundary. Narrative, NPC AI, Camera, Sound, UX, Technical Art and Architecture inspect independent evidence in parallel.

Synthesis: one Quest State authority, one NPC knowledge model, one Gameplay Event Contract and one Save Schema. Camera/SFX/UI consume events; they do not own quest or combat truth.

Wave 2: Content QA runs graph, state, save, localization and budget checks. QA Acceptance checks cross-domain contradictions and human test gaps.
