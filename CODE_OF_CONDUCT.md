# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, disclose uncertainty, credit sources, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sexualized conduct.

## Project-specific expectations

- Do not present unverified Agent output as fact.
- Do not use the project to copy another game's protected assets or distinctive content.
- Do not expose private projects, credentials, player data, or confidential reports.
- Discuss accessibility, culture, and player groups without stereotyping.
- Accept evidence-based review of prompts, workflows, and generated output.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report conduct privately, contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting contact and omit sensitive details.
